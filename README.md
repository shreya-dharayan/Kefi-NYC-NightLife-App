# Kefi — NYC Nightlife App

Vibe-matching app for NYC nightlife. Instead of searching by venue type ("bar," "club," "lounge"), Kefi matches people to spots based on the feeling they want tonight — chill and conversational, high-energy and dancing, a dressed-up date spot, or loud sports-bar chaos.

## Why

Search tools answer "what's near me" or "what's rated highly," not "where do I belong tonight." People fall back on texting the one friend who always knows where to go — Kefi structures that recommendation into a fast, re-runnable vibe check.

## Who it's for

Ages 21–30, NYC-based, goes out semi-regularly but doesn't have one default spot. Values atmosphere and crowd energy over price or proximity, and trusts friends over apps.

## Core features

- **Vibe Quiz** — a sub-minute, re-runnable quiz capturing tonight's energy level, group context, music preference, budget, and an optional "surprise me" toggle.
- **Match Flow** — 3–5 top matches as cards, with vibe tags ("good for dancing," "first-date friendly") and live signals (busy tonight, cover charge, line status).
- **Trending Feed** — real-time vibe-check posts from the community, the social layer that turns the tool into a habit.
